03 / Grammar reminder

Compare two things

Short adjectives

cold → colder

hot → hotter

friendly → friendlier

Long adjectives

dangerous → more dangerous

careful → more careful

stylish → more stylish

good → better   bad → worse

We often use than: A plane is faster than a car.

05 / Grammar reminder

Who is the best?

cold → the coldest

hot → the hottest

friendly → the friendliest

dangerous → the most dangerous

careful → the most careful

good → the best

bad → the worst

We use the with superlatives.

08 / Mini reading

Three friends, one trip

Anna is friendly and generous. She loves sunny weather, but she doesn't like dangerous sports.

Ben is serious and careful. He likes museums and cold weather.

Leo is stylish and friendly. He loves fast cars and hot weather.

The gifts

🎈 A hot-air balloon ride

🏎️ A sports car experience

🏛️ A museum day

Who would like each gift? Give a reason.

Anna: a balloon ride (if she feels safe). Ben: a museum day. Leo: a sports car experience. Other logical answers are possible.
09 / Writing

Make comparisons

Write full sentences. Use than.

1. A balloon ride is more dangerous than a museum visit.
2. Anna is friendlier than Ben. (Possible interpretation.)
3. A sunny day is better than a rainy day for a picnic.
10 / Challenge

Choose the best gift!

It's your friend's birthday. Your friend is careful, friendly and serious. They don't like dangerous activities. They love history.

Which gift is the best? Which is the worst? Why?

a museum ticket
a fast car ride
a flying lesson
🎁
I think a museum ticket is the best gift because my friend loves history. A flying lesson is more dangerous than a museum visit. A fast car ride isn't a good gift because my friend is careful.
